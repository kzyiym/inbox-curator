## Inbox Curator v1.0.2

Small compatibility fixes ahead of the Obsidian community plugin review.

### 🐛 Fixes

- **Minimum app version raised to `1.11.4`** — Required for the `SecretStorage` and `trashFile` APIs.
- Removed an unknown `icon` field from `manifest.json`.
- Replaced raw `h4`/`h5` heading elements with Obsidian's `Setting#setHeading()` in the settings UI.

**Requirements:** Obsidian `1.11.4`+ (desktop only)
